How to Play

Answer the question right to earn a serve. The ball drops down toward one of 3 lanes -- left, center, or right. Move your racket there with ⬅️/🎯/➡️ BEFORE it arrives, then tap SWING! right as it crosses into your zone (watch the glowing dashed box). Too early, too late, or the wrong side and you miss the return!
